# Gennaro-Brooks-Church.github.io
Gennaro Brooks-Church is the Founder and CEO of Cazimir and Brooks Energy, where he leads the development of AI solutions designed to transform underwriting and insurance operations. He is focused on helping insurance organizations capture institutional knowledge, streamline complex workflows, and improve decision-making through intelligent, learning-based technology.
Gennaro Brooks-Church works closely with insurers, brokers, and industry partners to build AI systems that become more accurate and valuable over time by learning from real-world underwriting decisions. His approach is centered on making artificial intelligence practical, transparent, and trustworthy, allowing businesses to improve efficiency without losing the expertise that drives quality underwriting.
Through his leadership, Gennaro Brooks-Church is helping shape the future of the insurance industry by developing technology that preserves institutional knowledge, supports underwriters in their daily decision-making, and empowers organizations to adapt confidently to an evolving industry landscape. His work reflects a clear belief that technology should enhance human expertise, not replace it, ensuring insurance organizations remain both innovative and grounded in experience.
